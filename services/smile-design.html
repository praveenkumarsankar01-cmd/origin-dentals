About smile design & veneers

Smile design starts with photos and a scan of your face and teeth. We design the new shape, length and shade on screen, then try it in your mouth as a temporary mock-up so you can see it, smile with it and change it.

Only when you are happy do we make the final veneers, bonding or crowns.

This may be suitable if you have

  • Chipped, worn or uneven front teeth
  • Small gaps you would like closed without braces
  • Teeth that whitening can't brighten enough
  • Old, discoloured fillings on front teeth

Frequently asked questions

Do veneers ruin your natural teeth?

Modern veneers need very little enamel removed — sometimes none, with bonding. We always choose the most conservative option that gives the result you want.

Will they look fake?

Not when they are designed around your face. That is what the mock-up stage is for: you approve the shape and shade before anything permanent happens.

Bonding or porcelain — which is better?

Bonding is quicker, cheaper and easy to repair; porcelain lasts longer and resists stains. We show you both side by side.
