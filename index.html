Inside the clinic

Modern equipment, explained simply

Equipment matters most when it makes treatment easier to understand. This is what we use, and what it means for you.

  1. 01

    Low-dose digital X-rays

    Images appear on screen in seconds, so we can show you exactly what we see. The dose is a fraction of that from old film X-rays.

  2. 02

    3D CBCT scans for implants

    Every implant is planned on a 3D scan of your jaw, so the position, any bone graft and the price are settled before treatment starts.

  3. 03

    Intraoral scanner

    A quick digital scan replaces impression trays for crowns, veneers and aligners, and gives you a 3D model of your own teeth.

  4. 04

    Magnification for root canals

    Root canals are carried out under magnification with rotary instruments, for cleaner canals and shorter appointments.

  5. 05

    Hospital-grade sterilisation

    Instruments are sterilised in a Class B autoclave and opened from sealed pouches in front of you.

Questions

Before your first visit

Answers to what patients most often ask us. For anything else, send us a message.

Ask on WhatsApp
Will treatment hurt?

Most treatment is done under local anaesthetic, and we apply numbing gel before any injection. You can raise a hand to pause at any time.

I'm nervous about dentists. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will give you a longer first appointment, explain each step before we do it, and stop whenever you ask.

How will I know what my treatment costs?

Starting prices are listed above. After your examination you receive an itemised written estimate, and nothing starts until you have agreed to it.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. EMI options are available for larger treatments such as implants, braces and clear aligners. Reception can give you the monthly breakdown.

Can I get advice before I travel to Chennai?

Yes. Send your X-rays or photos on WhatsApp and we will outline your options and likely costs, so you can plan your visit.

Is there parking near the clinic?

Street parking is available in front of the clinic, and we are two minutes' walk from Kodambakkam bus stop.
