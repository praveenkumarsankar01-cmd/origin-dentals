About the practice

Caring for Kodambakkam families since 2010

Origin Dentals opened in 2010 as a two-chair practice beside Kodambakkam bus stop. Today four specialists work from the same clinic and share one patient record, so the dentist treating you always has your full history.

  • Consultations in Tamil, English, Hindi, Telugu and Malayalam
  • Open until 9 PM on weekdays and on Sunday mornings
  • EMI options for larger treatments
