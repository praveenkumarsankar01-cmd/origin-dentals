About the clinic

A two-chair practice that grew into a specialist team

Origin Dentals opened in 2010 beside Kodambakkam bus stop. Families kept asking for the next thing — braces for the kids, an implant for a grandparent, a brighter smile for a wedding — and we grew around them instead of sending them across the city.

  • Five languages spokenTamil, English, Hindi, Telugu and Malayalam.
